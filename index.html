HOUSEHOLD · LABOR SUPPLY

maxcₜ, ℓₜU(cₜ, ℓₜ)
s.t.
cₜ + wₜℓₜ = wₜh + bₜ
cₜ ≥ 0,   0 ≤ ℓₜ ≤ h

Choices: current consumption cₜ and leisure ℓₜ. Given: wage wₜ, time endowment h, and nonlabor resources bₜ. Future income affects bₜ through lifetime wealth.

Interior optimality condition
Uℓ / Uc = wₜ

Labor supplied: Lₜˢ = h − ℓₜ.

FIRM · LABOR DEMAND

maxLₜAₜKₜαLₜ1−α − wₜLₜ
s.t.
Lₜ ≥ 0

Choice: current labor Lₜ. Given: Aₜ, the predetermined capital stock Kₜ, and wage wₜ. Output price is normalized to 1; costs independent of Lₜ are omitted.

Interior optimality condition
(1 − α)AₜKₜαLₜ−α = wₜ
MPLₜ = wₜ

HOUSEHOLD · SAVING

maxCₜ, Cₜ₊₁, sₜu(Cₜ) + βu(Cₜ₊₁)
s.t.
Cₜ + sₜ = Yₜ − Tₜ
Cₜ₊₁ = Yₜ₊₁ − Tₜ₊₁ + (1 + r)sₜ
Cₜ ≥ 0,   Cₜ₊₁ ≥ 0

Choices: consumption today Cₜ, consumption tomorrow Cₜ₊₁, and private saving sₜ. Given: current and expected future income and taxes, r, and discount factor β. Borrowing is allowed in this benchmark.

Interior optimality condition
u′(Cₜ) = β(1 + r)u′(Cₜ₊₁)

Private saving: sₜ = Yₜ − Tₜ − Cₜ.
National saving: Sₜ = sₜ + (Tₜ − Gₜ) = Yₜ − Cₜ − Gₜ.

FIRM · INVESTMENT

maxKₜ₊₁Aₜ₊₁Kₜ₊₁αLₜ₊₁1−α − (r + δ)Kₜ₊₁
s.t.
Kₜ₊₁ ≥ 0

Choice: next-period capital Kₜ₊₁. Given: expected Aₜ₊₁ and Lₜ₊₁, interest rate r, and depreciation rate δ. This is the reduced user-cost formulation: each unit of capital costs r + δ.

Interior optimality condition
αAₜ₊₁Kₜ₊₁α−1Lₜ₊₁1−α = r + δ
MPKₜ₊₁ = r + δ

Implied investment: Iₜ = Kₜ₊₁ − (1 − δ)Kₜ.

Curves are schematic; distances are illustrative. This is a sequence of comparative-static reasoning, not a time path. Each example starts from the same baseline. The permanent case assumes no net saving shift and a net increase in employment.
